AudioStack provides an AI-driven audio production platform that automates creation of broadcast‑ready audio for agencies, brands, ad‑tech platforms, radio, and video producers. Leveraging a library of 2,600+ voices and over 1,000 sonic assets, it can generate hyper‑local ads at scale, delivering thousands of ads in days with up to 90% faster turnaround. The platform offers embeddable APIs for dynamic creative, story engine, and instant creative solutions, supporting personalized audio content across multiple channels.
